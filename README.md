## Overview

Audiodrill gives language learners a practical way to study with authentic audio and video. Its browser-based workspace combines listening exercises, speaking activities, vocabulary work, and short repetition drills for independent learning, classroom use, or regular practice.

The application is centered on active repetition and learning from context. Practice can focus on a single word, a complete sentence, or a longer clip. Playback can be repeated at a comfortable pace, while translation and vocabulary-saving tools help build an organized collection of study material.

---

## What You Can Do

- Train listening skills using words, sentences, and extended audio passages
- Repeat audio or video sections in a loop and adjust playback speed
- Translate terms, look up vocabulary, and save useful items during a session
- Enter or dictate words and phrases, then hear them used in context
- Access live television channels and radio streams
- Practice pronunciation and natural rhythm with shadow reading
- Take part in voice-oriented games for a more interactive study experience
- Use the complete tool through a web browser

## Getting Started

An example Audiodrill workflow is:

1. Pick a listening or speaking exercise.
2. Choose a word, sentence, or section of media to study.
3. Replay the selection, reduce its speed, or keep it looping as needed.
4. Review translations and store vocabulary for future practice.
5. Move between audio, video, radio, and voice games to match your current study goal.

Short, regular sessions that combine repetition, translation, and shadow reading can help structure ongoing practice.
